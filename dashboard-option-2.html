Data Notes and Validation

Important considerations when interpreting the assessment results.

Standard Grade-Level Learner Reference

The most commonly reported learner totals are used as the standard grade-level reference:

  • Grade 7: 337
  • Grade 8: 394
  • Grade 9: 370
  • Grade 10: 355

Subjects Are Not Added Together

Learners are not totaled across learning areas because the same learners may appear in multiple subjects. Classification is therefore interpreted by grade and learning area rather than by adding learners across subjects.

Grade 7 Filipino and Mathematics

The supplied classification counts for Grade 7 Filipino and Mathematics total 286, while the reported grade population is 337. The remaining 51 learners are therefore displayed as Unclassified / Not Reported rather than being assigned to a performance category without evidence.

Science Grade 9 Correction

The corrected Science Grade 9 classification data is:

370 Total • 87 Advancing • 168 Benchmarking • 81 Connecting • 34 Developing • 0 Emerging

This corresponds to 23.51% Advancing, 45.41% Benchmarking, 21.89% Connecting, 9.19% Developing, and 0% Emerging.

MPS and Classification Average

The separate MPS dataset and learner classification dataset are retained as supplied. For Science Grade 9, the MPS table reports 83.82, while the classification table reports an average of 84.78. These figures are not automatically treated as errors because they belong to separate reported datasets.

No Emerging Learners Reported

After the Science Grade 9 correction, all supplied subject-grade records report zero learners in the Emerging category. This should be presented as a characteristic of the supplied data rather than interpreted as proof that no learning difficulties exist.
